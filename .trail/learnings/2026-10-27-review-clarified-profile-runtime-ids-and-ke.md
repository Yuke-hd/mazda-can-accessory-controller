## Decided
The identity contract excludes nonpersisted runtime_id assignments from semantic compatibility and retains them in artifact identity. Validated package-relative Source.path is provenance, retained in artifact identity and excluded from compatibility. Machine-specific temporary/absolute paths are outside the normalized schema. This corrects the source-path statement in the earlier identity note from this session.

## Tried and ruled out
Copying the full binding into compatibility included runtime_id and made local provider reassignment look like semantic incompatibility. Removing logical source paths from artifact identity hid attributable provenance changes. Independent review reproduced both issues; paired mutation regressions now require compatibility stability with an artifact change.

## Watch out
Golden preimages are literal expectations authored independently from canonical.py. The native golden excludes runtime_id in compatibility; expected literal bytes are hashed separately rather than regenerated by production identity code. Source comments change the source byte digest and artifact identity without changing resolved controller semantics.
