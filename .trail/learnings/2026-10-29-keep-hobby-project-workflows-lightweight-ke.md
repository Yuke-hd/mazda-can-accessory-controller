## Decided
The owner clarified on 2026-10-29 that this is a hobbyist project and does not need commercial-grade engineering. Scale recommendations and workflows accordingly. For AI-agent issues, prefer a short task brief containing the goal, scope, observable completion criteria, relevant checks, and references or dependencies only when useful.

## Tried and ruled out
The proposed 70-line issue template imposed excessive process through mandatory readiness states, authority fields, ownership coordination, criterion-to-evidence tables, and closure classifications. Do not make those universal requirements for small hobby-project tasks.

## Watch out
Keep task-specific requirements that affect actual correctness or existing CAN and lighting safety contracts. Add planning, coordination, or evidence detail only when the particular task needs it. This correction supersedes the earlier recommendation's level of formality, while retaining the fact that issues target AI coding agents.
