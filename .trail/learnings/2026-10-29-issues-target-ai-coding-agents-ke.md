## Decided
On 2026-10-29, the owner clarified that this repository's issues are meant for AI coding agents. Evaluate issue templates as bounded execution assignments: the agent needs authoritative contracts, explicit dependency outcomes, scope and ownership, observable acceptance criteria, and verification evidence. This clarification does not authorize implementing or rewriting existing issues.

## Tried and ruled out
A generic human bug-report form alone does not cover the repository's implementation and coordination work. Review of 181 issue bodies (20 open and 161 closed) also found canceled and consolidated closed issues, so closure alone is not proof that a dependency's required outcome shipped.

## Watch out
A default Markdown Agent task template and a separate lightweight epic tracker were recommended, not approved or installed. Keep current contracts in specifications and architecture documents. Child issues own their acceptance criteria. Separate automated, bench, and vehicle evidence and preserve the existing hardware authorization boundary.
